Frequently Asked Questions
How does campus delivery work?
Orders are brought directly to your hostel block reception or designated floor drop-point by student couriers.
Where do I collect counter pickup orders?
Present your 4-digit order token or QR code at the physical counter inside the student center.
What if an item is out of stock?
Merchants update real-time stock. If unavailable, you are immediately notified and refunded or offered a replacement.
How can I request items not listed in stores?
Use the "Item Requests" feature from your profile. Store managers review requests weekly to stock demanded items.
